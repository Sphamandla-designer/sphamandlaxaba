Design Process

Four stages, in order — from how the work happens today to something developers can build.

01 · Map

Understand the current state

Workflow analysis, current- and future-state journeys, and a stakeholder map showing where trust and handoffs break.

02 · Decide

Agree it before designing it

Assumptions become decisions logged against a named owner, settled in stakeholder sessions rather than on my judgment alone.

03 · Design

Principles before screens

Principles first, so the hard calls have something to answer to. Then IA, flows, and the states most work skips.

04 · Prove

Validate, then hand over

Working prototypes in front of the people who use and fund the work, then specs, tokens and a handoff session.

About

I design the parts of a product where the workflow is the hard part.

I'm Sphamandla Xaba, a Product Designer and UI/UX Designer based in Johannesburg, South Africa. Over three years I've designed enterprise software, websites and mobile applications — taking work from discovery and research through to developer handoff.

Most of what I do sits behind the interface: onboarding that has to earn trust, operational dashboards people live in all day, AI-assisted review where someone still has to be accountable for the result. The screens are the easy part.

My job is to make complexity legible — not to pretend it isn't there.

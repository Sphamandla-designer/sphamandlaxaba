03Approach

A thoughtful approach to designing digital products.

Good product design requires more than attractive interfaces. It requires understanding the problem, making informed decisions and creating experiences that work in practice.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Understand the product, business context, users and requirements.

    In practiceOn WasteMart, I started from four questions a driver must be able to answer at any moment — what now, what’s done, what’s left, what if it goes wrong. See it: Understand example →

  2. 02

    Structure

    Organise information, define navigation and map meaningful user journeys.

    In practiceManaGEM’s navigation is grouped the way the business runs — customers, sales, operations, admin — not the way its data is stored. See it: Structure example →

  3. 03

    Design

    Translate requirements into intuitive interfaces and interaction patterns.

    In practiceWasteMart’s primary button changes with the job’s state, so the next action is always the obvious one. See it: Design example →

  4. 04

    Refine

    Review the experience, resolve inconsistencies and improve usability.

    In practiceManaGEM’s statuses were brought into one plain-language set, so the same colour means the same thing in every module. See it: Refine example →

  5. 05

    Collaborate

    Work towards implementation through clear communication and collaboration.

    In practiceThe GEMIS website redesign was built by a development team working from my designs. See it: Collaborate example →

This is how I like to work. Not every project had room for every stage; each case study says what actually happened.

06 — About

Hi, I’m Sphamandla.

I’m a product designer in Johannesburg. I started out designing websites and landing pages for clients at a design studio, and now design enterprise software, mobile apps and websites at Gem Information Systems.

The work I enjoy most is the messy middle: taking a product with lots of moving parts and finding the structure that makes it feel simple. I like working close to developers, because a design only counts once it works in someone’s hands.

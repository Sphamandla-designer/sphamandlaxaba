Five Stages. You See The Work At The End Of Each One

The Same Process Every Project Runs, So Direction Is Agreed While It Is Still Cheap To Change.

  1. 01

    Discovery & Research

    Who the visitor is, what they came for, and what the business needs them to do. We look at your current site, your competitors and the words people actually use to search for what you sell.

    You get a brief, a scope and a timeline in writing.

  2. 02

    UX Architecture & SEO Mapping

    The shape of the thing: sitemap, page hierarchy, navigation, user journeys, and the search terms each page is meant to answer. This is where the site is decided, before anything is drawn.

    You get a sitemap, page hierarchy and journey map.

  3. 03

    Interface Design & Brand Integration

    The surface, drawn onto the structure. Responsive layouts for phone, tablet and desktop, a reusable component set, and your brand carried through rather than decorating the top of the page.

    You get designed pages across breakpoints, and a component set.

  4. 04

    Development & Optimisation

    Built on a CMS you can manage, with semantic markup, forms and integrations wired up, images optimised, and the metadata and internal linking put in as the pages are built.

    You get a working site on a staging link to review.

  5. 05

    Launch & Growth Monitoring

    Tested across devices and browsers, checked for broken links and redirects, then launched properly — followed by a handover with admin access and a walk-through of how to run it.

    You get the live site, the logins, and free maintenance to start.

Why The UX Comes First

A Website Is Not A Picture Of A Business. It Is How The Business Gets Understood

Visual design decides whether a visitor takes you seriously. Structure decides whether they find what they came for. Both matter, but only one of them can be fixed with a new colour scheme.

This is the part we will not skip. A layout drawn before the architecture is a guess, and it is the visitor who pays for it — by leaving.

See what we hold ourselves to

  • How fast the offer lands

    Whether someone understands what you sell in the first screen, or has to hunt for it.

  • Whether they believe you

    Clear structure, real work and plain language do more for credibility than a stock photo.

  • How easily they find the detail

    Pricing, scope, process, proof — reachable in one move, not buried three levels down.

  • How simple it is to make contact

    A visible path to an enquiry on every page, in the form the visitor prefers.

  • Whether it survives a phone

    Most of your traffic is mobile. A desktop layout squeezed down is not a mobile experience.

  • Where each page sends them next

    Every page has a job and a next step, so interest keeps moving instead of stopping.

Is This The Right Fit For You?

An Honest Read, Including The Cases Where It Is Not.

A good fit when

  • Your website is the first place a prospective client checks you out
  • You sell a service that takes explaining before anyone enquires
  • You are a professional firm and the site has to carry credibility
  • You are launching and need the structure right from the start
  • Your current site no longer matches what the business does
  • You want to manage the content yourself afterwards

Probably not yet when

  • You need a single page online this week and a template will do
  • The offer is still changing weekly and nothing is settled enough to structure
  • You want a site built to a design that is already finished and signed off elsewhere — the UX work is most of what we bring
  • You are looking for ongoing SEO or ads rather than a website

If that is where you are, say so. We would rather point you somewhere sensible than sell you a process you do not need yet.

How much does a website cost?

Packages start at R2,999 for a one to four page site, R5,999 for five to nine pages with UI/UX and basic SEO, and R8,999 for ten to sixteen pages with custom design and documentation. Each can be paid in full or split over three months. E-commerce and anything larger is quoted against scope.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the number of pages, whether the content is ready, and how quickly feedback comes back. We would rather give you a real date than a stock answer, so a timeline goes into your proposal once discovery has shown what the build involves. What we commit to upfront is a reply to any enquiry within two working days.

Do you design and develop, or only one of them?

Both, in the same process. The research, information architecture, interface design and build are done by the same studio, which is why the structure survives into the finished site instead of being reinterpreted by someone who was not in the discovery call.

Can you redesign a website I already have?

Yes, and it is a large part of what we do. We audit the current site, keep what is working, restructure what is not, and carry across the URLs you already rank for so a redesign does not reset your search history.

Can I update the website myself afterwards?

Yes. Sites are built on a CMS, so you can edit text, swap images and add pages without touching code or coming back to us. We hand over admin access and walk you through managing it.

Is SEO included?

The foundations are: semantic structure, heading hierarchy, page titles and descriptions, internal linking, image optimisation, an XML sitemap and a crawlable URL structure. Growth and Established packages add SEO optimisation and submission for indexing. Those foundations make a site findable; they do not decide where it ranks. Ongoing SEO — content, relevance and competing over time — is separate work.

Will it work properly on a phone?

Every package is responsive across phone, tablet and desktop, and we design the narrow layouts rather than shrinking the wide one. Type size, tap targets, navigation, forms and the enquiry action are all checked on real devices before launch.

Can you connect WhatsApp, forms and other tools?

Yes. Contact forms, email routing, WhatsApp enquiry journeys, maps, booking links and analytics are all normal parts of a build. Anything heavier than that we scope first, so it is priced honestly rather than discovered halfway through.

Do you provide maintenance after launch?

Every package includes free maintenance to start — one week on Starter, two on Growth, a month on Established. After that it is optional, from R399 to R1,200 a month depending on the package, and only charged if you ask for it. We do not lock anyone into a retainer to keep their own website running.

Do you only work with businesses in Johannesburg?

No. We are based in Johannesburg and work with clients in Pretoria, Cape Town and across South Africa. The whole process runs comfortably over calls and email, and plenty of our projects are delivered without a single meeting in a room.

What do you need from me to start?

Roughly what the business does, who you are trying to reach, what you want a visitor to do, and any brand material you already have. If your content is not written yet, say so early — it is usually the thing that decides the timeline.

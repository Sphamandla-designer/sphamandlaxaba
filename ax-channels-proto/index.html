What services do you offer?

Four, and they overlap: website design and development, digital product design, UI/UX design and UX strategy, and brand identity systems. Most projects start with one and draw on the others — a website usually needs the brand system behind it, and a product needs the UX work before any interface is drawn.

How much does a website cost?

Website packages start at R2,999 for a one to four page site, R5,999 for five to nine pages with basic SEO and UI/UX, and R8,999 for ten to sixteen pages with custom design and documentation. Each can be paid in full or split over three months. Product, UX and brand work is quoted against scope, because no two briefs are the same size.

How does a project actually run?

Five stages. We start with discovery and research, map the UX architecture and SEO, design the interface and integrate your brand, build and optimise, then launch and monitor. You see work at the end of each stage rather than only at the finish, so the direction is agreed as we go.

How long will my project take?

It depends on scope, and we would rather give you a real date than a stock answer. You get a timeline in writing with your proposal, once discovery has told us what the build actually involves. What we do commit to upfront is a reply to any enquiry within two working days.

Can I update the website myself afterwards?

Yes. Sites are built on a CMS, so you can edit text, swap images and add pages without coming back to us or touching any code. We hand over the site with the admin access and a walk-through of how to manage it.

Do you only work with businesses in Johannesburg?

No. We are based in Johannesburg and work with clients in Cape Town, Pretoria and across South Africa. The whole process runs comfortably over calls and email, and plenty of our projects are delivered without a single meeting in a room.

What happens after the site goes live?

Every package includes free maintenance after launch — one week on Starter, two on Growth, a month on Established. After that, ongoing maintenance is optional at R399 to R799 a month, and only charged if you ask for it. We do not lock anyone into a retainer to keep their own website running.

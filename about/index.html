About

Designing digital experiences with clarity, structure and purpose.

I’m Sphamandla Xaba, a product designer based in Johannesburg, South Africa. I design enterprise systems, mobile applications and websites — and I’m happiest when a product has a lot going on and needs someone to find the structure in it.

Alongside a BA in Environmental Studies & Geography from the University of the Witwatersrand, I’ve completed professional courses in UX design, design psychology and product design.

I started in 2023 at AX-Channels Studio, designing websites, landing pages and business platforms for clients, many of them built in WordPress. Working directly with clients taught me to turn loose business requirements into something concrete, and to explain design decisions in plain language.

Since 2025 I’ve been a UI/UX designer at Gem Information Systems, where I design enterprise software, internal business applications and websites. That’s where I’ve worked on the ManaGEM ERP and led the redesign of the GEMIS website.

02Design philosophy

What I hold myself to.

  1. Understand before designing

    What problem are we solving, for whom, and how do people work today? The answers decide the design more than any visual choice.

  2. Complex systems should feel simple

    Complexity belongs in the system, not in the user’s head. People should never have to wonder where they are or what happens next.

  3. Every interaction should build trust

    Especially in business software: show what the system did, let people check it, and make it easy to correct.

  4. Design for the build

    A small set of patterns that a team can actually build beats a beautiful one-off. I design with developers, not just for them.

01

Business context

GEMIS is a software company: it builds products such as the ManaGEM ERP, designs and builds enterprise platforms and websites for corporate clients, and runs an in-house product and interface design practice, GEMIS Studio.

The corporate website is the company’s main front door, so it has to explain a business that does several things — products, client software and design services — without becoming a list of everything at once.
